Platform Features

Nine integrated modules that turn raw deal documents into institutional-quality underwriting.

📄

OM Parsing

Upload a PDF offering memorandum and let AI extract the rent roll, operating statement, unit mix, and property details in seconds. Confidence scores flag anything that needs a second look.

📈

DCF Analysis

Auto-generate a discounted cash flow model with customizable hold period, discount rate, exit cap, and growth assumptions. Every cell is editable, and changes ripple through the full model in real time.

📊

Sensitivity Analysis

Stress-test your deal across two-variable matrices. Vary cap rate vs. rent growth, vacancy vs. exit cap, or any custom pair to see how returns shift under different market conditions.

📚

LP/GP Waterfall

Model preferred returns, catch-up provisions, and promote structures across unlimited tiers. Compare IRR and equity multiples for each partner class side by side.

🏢

Rent Roll Management

View, edit, and normalize unit-level lease data. Flag below-market rents, track lease expirations, and model rent bumps to see the impact on net operating income over your hold period.

📅

T12 Parsing

Extract trailing-twelve-month operating data from PDFs or spreadsheets. The engine normalizes line items, reconciles totals, and identifies outliers so your pro forma starts from clean actuals.

📝

Investment Memos

Generate LP-ready investment memos with deal summary, market context, financial highlights, risk factors, and return projections. Export as PDF or share a live link with your team.

Excel Export

Download a fully-formulated .xlsx workbook with live tabs for the DCF, rent roll, operating statement, sensitivity tables, and waterfall. Continue modeling in Excel, Sheets, or Numbers.

AI Deal Chat

Frequently Asked Questions

What property types does CRELYTIC Engine support?
CRELYTIC Engine supports all major commercial real estate property types including multifamily, office, retail, industrial, self-storage, mixed-use, and hospitality. The AI models are trained on thousands of OMs across every asset class to ensure accurate parsing and analysis regardless of property type.
How accurate is the OM parsing?
CRELYTIC Engine achieves 95-99% extraction accuracy on offering memorandums. The AI identifies and extracts rent rolls, operating statements, unit mixes, capital expenditure schedules, and property details. Every parsed field includes a confidence score, and you can review and correct any value before it flows into your underwriting model.
Can I customize the DCF assumptions and hold period?
Yes. Every assumption in the DCF is fully editable including hold period (1-30 years), discount rate, exit cap rate, rent growth, expense growth, vacancy, capital reserves, and financing terms. You can also save assumption templates for different property types or markets and apply them to new deals instantly.
What format is the Excel export?
Exports are generated as .xlsx files compatible with Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, and Apple Numbers. The workbook includes separate tabs for the DCF cash flow, rent roll, operating statement, sensitivity tables, waterfall distribution, and key metrics summary. All formulas are live so you can continue modeling in your preferred spreadsheet tool.
How does CRELYTIC Engine protect my deal data?
All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Your deal files are stored in isolated tenant environments and are never used to train AI models. CRELYTIC Engine runs on SOC 2-compliant infrastructure and supports single sign-on (SSO) for enterprise accounts. You can delete any deal and all associated data at any time.
