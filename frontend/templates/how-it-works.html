How It Works

Four steps. Three minutes. Complete underwriting.

1

Upload Your OM

Drag and drop a PDF offering memorandum or Excel rent roll into CRELYTIC Engine. Supports documents from any brokerage format—CBRE, JLL, Cushman & Wakefield, Marcus & Millichap, or any custom layout. No templates required.

2

AI Extracts Your Data

Claude AI reads the entire document and extracts 100+ data fields: property details, financial metrics, tenant information, lease terms, and operating expenses. Each extracted value includes a confidence score so you know exactly what to verify.

3

Review & Customize

Review the extracted data in a clean interface. Adjust key assumptions—cap rates, rent growth, vacancy, expense ratios—and configure your equity waterfall structure with custom promote tiers and preferred returns.

4

Analyze & Export

Review the full DCF projection, sensitivity tables across cap rate and growth scenarios, and waterfall distributions for each partner tier. Export your complete analysis to Excel with live formulas, a PDF investment memo, or a Word document.
